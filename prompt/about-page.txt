Introduction to my work and therapeutic philosophy
I'm a licensed clinical psychologist, working with individuals across different life stages who are navigating emotional challenges, life transitions, or internal struggles that may be difficult to articulate.
In my therapeutic work, I integrate cognitive-behavioral therapy (CBT), acceptance and commitment therapy (ACT), and psychodynamic approaches — tailoring the process to each person's needs. I see therapy as a collaborative space that allows room for vulnerability, emotional connection, responsibility, and growth.

My areas of focus include trauma and post-trauma, anxiety, depression, grief and loss, self-esteem, relationships, parenting, and women’s health.

Professional Experience:

Over the years, I have accompanied individuals through diverse and complex life situations, combining in-depth academic knowledge with broad clinical experience. My professional path began with a Master’s degree in Clinical Psychology, which I completed with honors, driven by a genuine commitment to my profession and to the people I work with.
I completed my clinical internship in a Day Hospital unit, where I worked with patients struggling with depression, anxiety, and trauma. The daily encounters with individuals in deep distress provided me with valuable clinical tools and a wide understanding of how to offer support even in the most difficult moments. At the same time, I gained extensive experience in facilitating therapeutic groups.
During the COVID-19 pandemic, a time of widespread psychological distress, I responded to the urgent social need by providing short-term interventions that offered focused and immediate support to those in crisis. Later on, I worked in the Women’s Health Department, where I met women and couples facing complex challenges such as infertility, anxiety, postpartum depression, pregnancy loss, and stillbirth. This experience deepened my awareness of the importance of giving space to voices and emotional worlds that are not always expressed or acknowledged.
As part of my professional training, I further specialized in trauma-focused therapy, providing tailored treatment to individuals carrying painful experiences who required sensitive and precise care. In continuation of this work, I also took part in an emergency response mission, delivering immediate and focused psychological support to evacuees following the events of October 7th. During this challenging period, I also provided short-term, online interventions for medical teams in hospitals and health clinics—sessions that were vital for sustaining the resilience of those on the frontlines.
