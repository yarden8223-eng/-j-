In my clinic, I work with individuals of various ages who are dealing with a range of psychological difficulties — including anxiety, trauma, depression, grief and loss, low self-esteem, complex relationships, and more.
I believe that each person needs a therapeutic space tailored to them, and therefore I draw from a variety of approaches — including cognitive-behavioral therapy (CBT), acceptance and commitment therapy (ACT), and at times, psychodynamic work.
To me, the core of therapy lies in meeting the difficulty with compassion, while fostering emotional connection, choice, and personal responsibility.

בקליניקה שלי אני פוגשת אנשים ונשים בגילאים שונים, המתמודדות עם מגוון קשיים נפשיים – מצבי חרדה, טראומה, דיכאון, אובדן ואבל, דימוי עצמי נמוך, יחסים מורכבים ועוד.
