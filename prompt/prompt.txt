What can I expect in the first session?
The first session is an opportunity for us to get to know each other. We’ll talk about what brings you to therapy, what you’d like help with, and what you hope to gain from the process. I’ll also ask some questions to better understand your background and current experience. Most importantly, this meeting helps us decide together whether this feels like a good fit for you.

How long does each session last?
Each session lasts 45 minutes.

How often do we meet?
In most cases, therapy sessions take place once a week, on the same day and time. Depending on your needs and preferences, we can occasionally adjust the frequency.

How long does therapy usually take?
The length of therapy varies from person to person. Some people come for a few months to work through a specific issue, while others engage in longer-term work. We'll regularly review the process together and adapt it to suit your goals and pace.

What types of therapy do you offer?
My work draws from several therapeutic approaches, including CBT (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy), ACT (Acceptance and commitment therapy), trauma-informed care, and elements of psychodynamic therapy . The exact approach depends on your unique needs — we’ll tailor the process to fit you.

Do you offer in-person and/or online sessions?
Yes, I offer both in-person sessions at my clinic and online sessions via a secure video platform. You're welcome to choose what feels most comfortable and accessible for you.

What is the cost per session?
The session fee will be discussed during our initial contact. I aim to keep the process transparent and respectful, and I'm happy to provide more details by phone or email.

Do you work with health insurance or provide reimbursement receipts?
I'm a licensed clinical psychologist, and I provide receipts for private insurance reimbursement where applicable. Some supplementary health insurance plans (פוליסות פרטיות) offer partial refunds — I recommend checking directly with your provider.
In addition, I am listed by the National Insurance Institute (Bituach Leumi) as a psychologist authorized to provide treatment for individuals recognized as victims of hostilities, including terror- or war-related incidents. If this applies to you, I’ll be glad to assist with the necessary process and documentation.

What is your cancellation policy?
If you need to cancel or reschedule a session, I ask for at least 24 hours' notice. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours’ notice are charged at the full session fee.

Is therapy confidential?
Yes. Everything discussed in therapy is kept strictly confidential, in accordance with the ethical guidelines of the Ministry of Health and the Israeli Psychological Association. There are rare exceptions related to safety concerns, which I’ll explain in our first meeting.

Where is your office located?
My clinic is located in Ramat Gan. The exact address will be shared once we schedule a session.

Is the space accessible (e.g., wheelchair accessible)?
Yes, the clinic is fully wheelchair accessible.

How do I schedule an appointment?
You can contact me via the Contact page on this site, by email, or by phone. I’ll get back to you as soon as possible to schedule an initial consultation.

Do you offer sessions in other languages? (e.g., English/Hebrew)
Yes, I offer therapy in both Hebrew and English.

What if I’m not sure therapy is right for me?
That’s completely okay — many people feel uncertain at first. The initial sessions are a space to explore whether this feels helpful and whether we’re a good match. There’s no pressure or obligation to continue if it doesn’t feel right for you.

Can I contact you between sessions if needed?
You’re welcome to contact me between sessions for practical matters (like scheduling or cancellations). For emotional support, we’ll try to keep that within the therapy space, but we can explore together what kind of contact feels supportive and appropriate.

Is it normal to feel nervous about starting therapy?
Absolutely. Many people feel anxious before beginning therapy — it’s a new and unfamiliar process. That’s a completely valid feeling, and you're welcome to talk about it in our first session. Taking the first step is often the hardest part.

What if I don’t know what I want to talk about?
That’s very common, and it’s completely okay. You don’t need to come with a plan — we’ll explore together what’s on your mind, what you’re feeling, or even what’s missing. Therapy can be just as valuable when things feel vague or stuck.

Can therapy help even if I’ve tried it before and it didn’t work?
Yes. Every therapeutic relationship is unique, and what didn’t work before might work differently in a new setting. I encourage you to share your past experience — it can help us create a process that better meets your needs.

במרבית המקרים, הפגישות מתקיימות אחת לשבוע, באותו יום ובאותה שעה. עם זאת, בהתאם לצרכים ולהעדפות שלך, ניתן לעיתים לשנות את התדירות.

אורך הטיפול משתנה מאדם לאדם. יש מי שמגיעים למספר חודשים כדי לעבוד על נושא ממוקד, ואחרים בוחרים בתהליך ארוך טווח. לאורך הדרך נבחן יחד את ההתקדמות ונראה כיצד להתאים את הטיפול לקצב ולמטרות שלך.

מה עלות כל מפגש?
שכר הטיפול נמסר במהלך יצירת הקשר הראשוני. אני שמה דגש על תהליך שקוף ומכבד, ואשמח למסור פרטים נוספים בטלפון או במייל.

האם את עובדת עם קופות חולים או ביטוחים פרטיים?
אני פסיכולוגית קלינית מומחית, ומספקת קבלות להחזר ביטוחי (במקרים בהם הפוליסה מאפשרת זאת). חלק מהביטוחים המשלימים מציעים החזר חלקי – מומלץ לבדוק ישירות מול ספק הביטוח שלך.

בנוסף, אני רשומה במוסד לביטוח לאומי כפסיכולוגית מורשית לטיפול בנפגעי פעולות איבה או מלחמה. אם את.ה מוכר.ת כנפגע.ת איבה, אוכל לסייע בתהליך ובהגשת המסמכים הנדרשים.

• I want the section to include subtle, elegant animations that bring the page to life — for example, text fade-ins, smooth scrolling effects, elements sliding in as you scroll, or hover animations on buttons or links. The animations should support a calm and professional tone, not be overwhelming.
• Keep it minimal and soothing: Prioritize clarity, whitespace, and simple navigation. Avoid information overload—each section should be concise, with the option to read more if desired.
• Focused content per page: Every page should include only the essential information, presented in a way that’s easy to scan (e.g., short paragraphs, bullet points, headings).
• Use expandable sections (like accordions) for longer content such as FAQs or course details, so visitors can open only what they’re interested in.
• Visual balance: Combine text with calming visuals—use imagery to break up sections and support the content emotionally, not distract from it.
